★ Featured Spotlight 🌿 Ecology & Landscape Genetics bioRxiv 2026

Connectivity and dispersal mode shape the landscape genetics of a carnivorous pitcher plant-arthropod metacommunity

Authors: Nonno Hasegawa, Asa E. Conover, Matin Miryeganeh, David W. Armitage

Organism: Darlingtonia californica (Cobra Lily)
Genomic Data: 19,166 nuclear SNPs (199 individuals)
ADAMIXTURE: Tested K = 2–15 · 5-fold CV (Optimal K = 3–4)

Investigated how landscape resistance and species-specific dispersal drive genetic structure in a tripartite mutualistic system across Oregon and Northern California. Using ADAMIXTURE with 5-fold cross-validation, the authors resolved sharp regional population boundaries, demonstrating that gene flow in the carnivorous host is restricted by landscape barriers rather than simple geographic distance.

ADAMIXTURE: Adaptive first-order optimization for biobank-scale genetic clustering

Authors: Joan Saurina-i-Ricos, Daniel Mas Montserrat, Alexander G. Ioannidis

Scale: 500,000 Individuals × 500,000 SNPs
Speed: < 2 Hours on single GPU (vs days)
Optimization: Adam-EM & Accelerated Quasi-Newton (BR-QN)

The foundational paper introducing ADAMIXTURE. Demonstrates an order-of-magnitude leap in speed and scalability over traditional quasi-Newton algorithms, unlocking routine unsupervised admixture inference on full biobank-scale cohorts without downsampling.
